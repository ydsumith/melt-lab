Graduate assistantships and scholarships

Application Information

Background

Students with preparation in mechanical engineering, thermofluids, biomedical engineering, scientific computing, molecular simulation, data analysis, or related fields may be a fit, depending on available projects.

Useful Skills

Relevant skills may include Python, MATLAB, molecular dynamics, LAMMPS, NAMD, high-performance computing, numerical methods, CAD, or experimental data analysis.

What to Send

Please include a resume or CV, unofficial transcript, brief research-interest statement, and any relevant project or code samples. Preferred email subject: Prospective MELT Researcher - [Degree Level] - [Your Name].
